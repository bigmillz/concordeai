**ConcordeAI 6.0.3**

- **A cloud key that runs out of budget never becomes the answer** — its notice is treated as a failure, that provider rests for an hour, and the next option answers instead, down to the models on this Mac. You always get a reply.
- **Three update channels** — Stable, Beta and Nightly, chosen in Settings › About. Nightlies are rebuilt on every change and named by their commit; betas are numbered per version, the macOS way.
- **The post-update card** names exactly what you were on and lists what changed in this build.
- **The disk image** shows the true build label and sets the wordmark in the app's own face.
- **Version line** lives at the top of Settings only.
