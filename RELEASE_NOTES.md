**ConcordeAI 7.0 beta 3**

The new look, all the way through, and the first signed release.

- **A darker, deeper sky.** Near-black, with three layers of stars and the background footage tinted to the time of day. Calm stays still.
- **A level aurora.** It drifts slowly from side to side, with a soft, wavy lower edge, and never runs into the box or behind your words.
- **A cleaner home.** Just the greeting, the question box and the sky. Your recent chats are in the sidebar.
- **A flatter box.** The question box is lower and slimmer. The send key stays plain until you type.
- **Chat, Code and Funnels in one place,** a sliding switch at the top. The tabs in the sidebar are gone.
- **Settings and updates at the top right.** A gear opens Settings, and "Update ready" appears beside it when there's a new version. The top bar stays tidy at any window size.
- **Answers settle in like ink.** Each word arrives a little lighter and settles in, and nothing jumps while it writes.
- **Ultra has a moment.** Slide to Ultra and the sky holds its breath, then the box lights up and stars rise and hang above it. While you stay on Ultra, the bar shimmers and star patterns come and go.
- **Signed and notarized by Apple.** On a Mac it opens like any other app, with no warning.
- **Fixed:** every answer ended with "Couldn't send". Answers land properly again.

On Windows, the first open asks once: More info › Run anyway.
