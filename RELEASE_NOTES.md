**ConcordeAI 7.0 beta 2**

A smoother slider, a livelier Ultra, and a steadier app.

- **A slimmer effort slider.** It's a thin, fluid bar again: drag it and it follows your hand, let go and it settles. Calmer colour too, silver up to Extra, ice to violet at Ultra.
- **Ultra lands.** Slide to Ultra and the bar charges, the top of the box lights up and stars rise out of it. Then it stays quietly alive: a slow light along the box, stars drifting above it, now and then a shooting star. The spinning star on the slider is gone.
- **Calmer sending:** no flashing rings; smoother backgrounds.
- **Settings match the new look.** Same layout, same glass. Updates read Stable, Beta and Nightly.
- **⌘D hold-to-talk stops when you let go,** even when the microphone is slow to start.
- **The Mac app opens faster,** with no blank window, and if its engine crashes it starts it again by itself.
- **The window remembers its size** and where you left it.

First open on a Mac: System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. On Windows: More info › Run anyway.
