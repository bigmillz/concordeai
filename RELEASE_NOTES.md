**ConcordeAI 7.0 beta 4**

The look, settled, and a lot more dependable.

- **A new window on Mac, written in C++.** Lighter and quicker on its feet than the old one, with the same look, your chats and settings carried over as they are.

- **A cleaner home.** A near-black sky with a soft aurora above the greeting, a graphite question box, and your three latest chats right under it.
- **Answers that read cleanly.** Crisp text that streams in smoothly. Lists, nested lists, tables, task lists, footnotes and long links now come out right.
- **Fewer wasted searches.** Sums, how-tos and questions about the chat itself get answered directly. A follow-up keeps its topic; a new question starts fresh.
- **Straight answers.** No more "I didn't find…" when the answer is known.
- **Honest when things go wrong.** A cut-off answer says "The connection dropped." with Continue and Try again. No internet? It says so plainly. Damaged model files? Another model answers, and you can download them again.
- **Send stays Send.** Start a new chat while an answer is still writing: it finishes in the background, and your new question waits its turn.
- **Code shows its work.** Every command is listed with its state. Anything that changes your server waits for your OK, and risky commands ask one at a time.
- **Funnels, step by step.** One clear flow, your picks collected at the top, and a final pick that sticks to the sizes and numbers you chose.
- **Dictation that keeps up.** Press ⌘D (Ctrl+D on a PC) and talk; it stops on its own when you pause. Pick its language in Settings.
- **A calmer Settings.** Five short sections in a dark, translucent sheet, one line per setting, the details a click away.
- **Tidier everywhere.** A ⋯ menu on every chat and a top bar that fits any window.
- **Ultra goes further.** More stars, a slow twist through the aurora, and a moment when you slide to Ultra. Everyday levels stay calm.
- **Quiet nights.** A sleeping server isn't woken overnight unless you picked it alone. Wake up server in Settings still works.

Signed and notarized: on a Mac it opens like any other app, and updates are checked against the signature before they install. On Windows: More info › Run anyway.
