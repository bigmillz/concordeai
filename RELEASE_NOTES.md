**ConcordeAI 6.0.1** — a fast follow-up to 6.0.

**Fixed**
- Funnel recommendations could fail to appear at all after the final question — the verdict step now always answers (this only affected builds after 6.0's release, but the safeguard ships for everyone)
- The titlebar logo is back, centered on the window and staying centered as you resize

**Sharper**
- Places run on their own clock even when your Mac is set to another timezone, with your home area as the default for any local question
- "This weekend" means the coming Saturday and Sunday, with both days forecast
- Venue answers carry closure evidence when it can be found, and hours that come only from a venue's own website are labeled "listed, unverified"
- What's-playing questions search Rotten Tomatoes, Fandango and Box Office Mojo first; current prices must carry a date and source
- Funnel verdicts are audited for what your picks actually mean, with exact product names or none
- Chat answers keep their numbers consistent and never invent "most people" statistics
