**6.1 RC1 — the ConcordeAI rename, ready for a real shakedown.** Everything below is in; what's left before 6.1 proper is signing in and syncing your chats between machines.

- **The wordmark is finished.** ConcordeAI everywhere, with the **AI** set extra bold — in the sidebar, Settings, the setup wizard, the sign-in door, the app icon, and now the title bar itself, which is one seamless dark surface instead of a pale macOS strip.
- **Models got a proper pane.** A scrolling list of every model with what it's for, install and remove on each row, and a Manage panel that shows what you have installed and how much space it takes — then offers Minimum, Recommended, Full, or Max. Recommended keeps one of each kind at its newest version instead of hoarding superseded ones.
- **Waiting feels honest.** The progress bar glides, tells you roughly how long is left, and stays hidden entirely on quick answers. Once a first draft is ready, **Answer now** takes the fast answer instead of the polished one.
- **It checks for updates on its own**, hourly, so leaving the app open no longer means falling behind.
- **Your account, plainly stated.** A new Account pane shows how you're signed in and where the exits are — and Forget Me now asks what to erase, checks it's you, and makes you type the words.
- **Funnels accept your own answer** instead of dropping you into generic advice when the option cards don't fit.

Under the hood: a security fix worth naming — any web page you visited could previously have told this app, running on your own machine, to delete things. It can't any more.
