**ConcordeAI 6.0** — the release. Everything since the betas, judged fifteen automated quality cycles deep.

**Answers you can act on**
- Places run on their own clock: "open now near williamsburg" is computed on Brooklyn's time even when your Mac is set to another timezone
- Local questions stay local (Brooklyn, not Virginia), open-now verdicts name real venues with cited hours or a real reopen time, and "this weekend" means the coming one
- Weather has two live sources, refuses stale or impossible readings, and never invents a forecast
- Funnels recommend real, specific things, honor what you clicked, and a second model audits every verdict before you see it
- Chat answers keep a friend's shape — prose and short lists, no walls of tables — and use what the app remembers about you plainly or not at all

**A cleaner app**
- Six redundant models retired; an auto-clean switch (and a Clean up now button) reclaims space from superseded versions
- Search your past chats from the sidebar — titles instantly, full text after three characters
- The hero greets you by name and hometown once you set them in Settings
- Centered titlebar lockup, one wordmark weight everywhere, the version quietly under the system monitor, remove links only in manage mode
- Fixed: pages could stay cached after an update
