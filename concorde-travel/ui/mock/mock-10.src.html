    Is it cheaper from somewhere else?

    The same seat is often filed at a different fare depending on the country the ticket is sold in. Ask, and the search re-runs under other points of sale, no VPN, and says what the difference is worth after the catches.

    How the grade works

    Every flight is priced the same way: the ticket, the bag you carry, the ride from your door and to theirs at that hour, your time at a rate, and small charges for what makes a trip worse. One number, in dollars, with every line shown. The letter never moves. The order is yours.
